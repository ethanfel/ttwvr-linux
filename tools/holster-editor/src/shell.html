FIELD MANUAL

Configure. Export. Equip.

  1. Quit Fallout and your mod manager. This tool edits a copy; the game writes its state when it closes.
  2. Load TTWVR.state. It contains the numbered holsters and their anchors. A savegame or TTWVR.ini is a different file.
  3. Choose a slot and an anchor. Numbers match the game’s 1–8 hotkeys. Drag its numbered centre to position it; drag the square handle to resize its grab sphere. Use Front for left/right and Side for depth. Both views adjust height. Arrow keys move 1 cm (Shift: 5 cm). Numeric controls remain available.
  4. Export the file. Keep a backup of the original and replace it with the downloaded copy. Its filename must be TTWVR.state, without a browser-added “(1)”.
  5. Launch the game and check the fit. The preview uses an approximate reference body and pose. Fine placement depends on your avatar and calibration.

Mirror a holster

Choose a different destination slot under Mirror to another slot, then press the mirror button. Right upper arm becomes left upper arm, for example, with the matching local offset and grab radius. The destination is replaced in one undoable action. Subsequent edits are independent; this does not link the slots. Automatic slots mirror their saved body-relative position but have no bone preview until you choose an anchor.

Where is the file?

Windows — your Documents folder, which may be redirected to OneDrive:

My Games\FalloutNV\TTWVR.state

Linux / Proton — inside the prefix used by your VR profile:

drive_c/users/steamuser/Documents/My Games/FalloutNV/TTWVR.state

TTWVR stores this configuration in New Vegas’s FalloutNV folder. Place a holster in VR and quit once if the holster sections have not been created yet.

What gets edited?

Only holster records in [SavedSlots] and [SavedSlotsOn]. Other sections, encoding and line endings are preserved. This does not assign weapons to hotkeys or change a savegame.

Automatic removes an explicit bone binding. TTWVR then chooses a nearby bone from the saved body-relative position. New explicit bindings start at a zero local offset; adjust the offset or finish placement in VR.

Runs entirely offline. No installation, uploads, runtime libraries or network connection required. Original tool code: GPL-3.0-only.
