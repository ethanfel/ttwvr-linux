    FIELD MANUAL

    Configure. Export. Equip.

    1. Quit Fallout and your mod manager. This tool edits a copy; the game writes its state when it closes.
    2. Load TTWVR.state. It contains the numbered holsters and their anchors. A savegame or TTWVR.ini is a different file.
    3. Choose a preview pose. Select Seated · desk chair to check reach around the seat and armrests. Switching pose only changes the preview. Choose Selected placement or Enabled layout and press Auto adapt to fit grab spheres to the skin. After changing weight, press it again to bring loose placements in or push embedded placements out. One Undo restores the previous positions.
    4. Choose a slot and an anchor. Numbers match the game’s 1–8 hotkeys. Drag its numbered centre to position it; drag the square handle to resize its grab sphere. Use Front for left/right and Side for depth. Both views adjust height. Arrow keys move 1 cm (Shift: 5 cm). Numeric controls remain available.
    5. Export the file. Keep a backup of the original and replace it with the downloaded copy. Its filename must be TTWVR.state, without a browser-added “(1)”.
    6. Launch the game and check the fit. The preview uses an approximate reference body and pose. Fine placement depends on your avatar and calibration.

    Ammo pouch

    Select Ammo pouch below the eight slots. In 3D, Body outline shows the approximate skin surface around the skeleton. Use Estimated body weight to preview a slimmer or larger build at the same reference height. Drag the background to orbit and drag the R, F or U arrow to move right, forward or up. You can also drag P in Front and Side or enter its right, forward and floor height in centimetres. If your file has no saved pouch, choose Set initial position to place one. Undo or Reset pouch restores the loaded placement.

    Start from a preset

    Preset layouts offers seven belt, vest and holster layouts, from four to eight enabled holsters. The P marks the ammo pouch. Choose its side and preview its reach before applying. One export saves the pouch and holsters together. Utility rig puts one forearm target opposite the selected Pip-Boy arm; this remains independent of pair order. One Undo restores your previous layout. Slots outside the chosen layout are disabled while their positions are retained. Visibility, calibration and other state sections stay as loaded. Export and check your reach in VR.

    Body holsters behind you?

    Field Kit 1.1–1.3 reversed front and back in the preview. If you exported body placements with those versions, this moves centres behind the reference body to the front. It affects pelvis, chest and leg anchors, including disabled slots; arms and Automatic slots stay unchanged. Intentionally rear-mounted body holsters would also move. Review the Side view, then export. One Undo restores the layout. Loading a file never repairs it automatically.

    Mirror a holster

    Choose a different destination slot under Mirror to another slot, then press the mirror button. Right upper arm becomes left upper arm, for example, with the matching local offset and grab radius. The destination is replaced in one undoable action. Subsequent edits are independent; this does not link the slots. Automatic slots show and mirror their saved position relative to the eyes. TTWVR chooses the nearest bone when the avatar loads.

    Where is the file?

    Windows — your Documents folder, which may be redirected to OneDrive:

    My Games\FalloutNV\TTWVR.state

    Linux / Proton — inside the prefix used by your VR profile:

    drive_c/users/steamuser/Documents/My Games/FalloutNV/TTWVR.state

    TTWVR stores this configuration in New Vegas’s FalloutNV folder. Place a holster in VR and quit once if the holster sections have not been created yet.

    What gets edited?

    Holsters use [SavedSlots] and [SavedSlotsOn]. TTWVR 1.1.8 stores its ammo pouch and Stimpak circle in [SavedSpots]. Older layouts use [SavedPouch]. To start using the new spots on an older layout, select the ammo pouch and choose Use 1.1.8 equipment spots. Select Ammo pouch to change its body anchor or grab radius. Other sections, encoding and line endings are preserved. This does not assign weapons to hotkeys or change a savegame.

    Automatic removes an explicit bone binding. TTWVR then chooses a nearby bone from the saved body-relative position. New explicit bindings start at a zero local offset; adjust the offset or finish placement in VR.

    Runs entirely offline. No installation, uploads, runtime libraries or network connection required. Original tool code: GPL-3.0-only.
